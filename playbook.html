Oxagen house system · v2

One house.
Two names. One gold.

Everything a customer sees from Oxagen and Stella comes from one system: Space Grotesk, warm ink and paper, and a single gold that each name carries in exactly one glyph.

The decision

Oxagen's kit is the house kit

The Oxagen brand kit already had the right bones: Space Grotesk, a warm near-black and a warm off-white, and a wordmark whose only colour is one gold letter. This system takes that kit as the base and brings Stella onto it.

oxagen is the kit's wordmark, reproduced from the font: the word in Space Grotesk 600, lowercase, its x in gold. stella* is the same word treatment followed by the font's own asterisk, in the same gold. The asterisk is a character, not a drawing; it was never redrawn, and it must not be.

The gold is the kit's Bronze Gold lifted one step: a little lighter, a little richer, a few degrees toward yellow, so it reads as gold rather than copper. Both names use it at the same value, so the two marks cannot drift apart.

  • One glyph is gold. The x in oxagen, the asterisk in stella. Never a second one.
  • Gold is identity, and one action per screen. It is never a surface, and it never means a state.
  • Gold as text on paper becomes gold-deep. The mark keeps its metal; words do not.
  • Nothing sits to the left of stella. The asterisk is the only mark. Oxagen's lockup is the one exception, and only for Oxagen.
  • The icon is the only picture we own. No stock illustration, no gradient mesh, no 3D render. A surface that needs an image uses a bigger icon.

Logos

Two wordmarks, one em

Both are set at the same size, 131.7 px per em, the size the kit's oxagen outline was frozen at. Each sits in an ink-tight box, so oxagen (454×93) and stella* (406×94) are the same height to within a pixel and the same letter size exactly.

The asterisk

Space Grotesk's asterisk is a six-armed, cap-height glyph, 59 units square at the logo em. It sits where the font puts it after an a, with no added space and no kerning. It is the same glyph in the wordmark, the icon, the favicon and the wallpaper.

Variants that ship

The oxagen lockup

Oxagen alone has a lockup: the monogram at four thirds of the wordmark's height, a gap of a fifth, then the word. Use it where a square mark and a name are needed together. Stella has no lockup; its asterisk is already in the word.

Minimum sizes

Wordmark 88 px wide on screen, 24 mm in print. Icon 24 px, 8 mm. Lockup 120 px, 32 mm. Below that, use the favicon.

Icons

The asterisk and the ox

Where a square is required, Stella uses its asterisk and Oxagen uses the kit's continuous ox: an open loop whose right edge is absorbed into an asymmetric x. Gold appears only on the two short segments the loop and the x share, so the symbol stays one gesture. It must not be rebuilt as a circle with a cross laid over it.

At 16 to 48 px the ox monogram does not survive, so Oxagen's favicon is the kit's simplified gold x alone. Stella's asterisk survives as itself.

Colour

One metal, two grounds

The gold is derived, not picked: the kit's Bronze Gold moved in OKLCH by +0.043 lightness, +0.014 chroma and +1.8° hue. The build fails if the pinned value stops matching that derivation. gold clears 7.5:1 on ink; gold-deep clears 4.9:1 on paper, which is why gold as text on paper becomes gold-deep.

Type

Space Grotesk, four weights

One family for both brands, in the product, on the site, in print and in the logos. 600 is the logo weight. 700 for display, 600 for headings, 500 for interface, 400 for reading. The scale is a 1.2 ratio from 16 px.

700 · displayIt does not say done. It proves it.
600 · headingVerified work becomes owned capability.
500 · interfaceRun pipeline · Witness authored · Verdict confirmed
400 · bodyA green check is not an answer. The agent writes the test that would have caught the bug, watches it fail, then makes it pass.
ligaturesfi fl · verified · office · flow

Code and terminal output stay in the system monospace. Space Grotesk is not a code face and is never used for one.

Motion

Light passes over the metal

The house motion is the shimmer: a band of light crosses the gold glyph every 2.8 seconds. Stella's asterisk turns a sixth of a turn, its own symmetry, as the light passes. Oxagen's monogram draws itself on, the way the kit animates it, and the gold arrives last. All of it is CSS inside the SVG; it runs in an <img> with no script, and prefers-reduced-motion lands it on a still mark.

Surfaces

One composition rule

A surface is a ground, one warm bloom of the metal, one oversized ghost of the brand's own icon placed off-centre, and at most a few lines of type. Every surface below is the vector the kit ships, not a screenshot.

Files

Every pixel is generated

No file in this kit is drawn by hand. Every PNG is a render of the SVG beside it; every SVG is emitted from build/. The colours live in one file, build/color.py, so a change there moves every asset on the next run.
